Reviewed source finding — Day One Privacy & Security FAQs, 2026-08-13

Publicly stated patterns: an option to stay local without an account; a clear explanation of where data and encryption keys reside; encryption during sync; passcode and biometric safeguards; concealed content; and data export choices. Imanote takeaway: add a “privacy centre” that plainly says “stored on this device,” displays the last backup date, offers optional biometric unlock, supports discreet notification text, and makes encrypted export/restoration understandable.
